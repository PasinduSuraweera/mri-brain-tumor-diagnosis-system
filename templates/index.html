What to upload

  • A single 2D axial brain MRI slice (T1, T1 contrast or T2), exported as an image.
  • Uncropped, with the black background around the head, and without annotations or colour overlays.
  • The model only knows four classes: glioma, meningioma, pituitary tumor and no tumor.
